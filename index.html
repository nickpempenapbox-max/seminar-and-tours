Organizer: Cisco Networking Academy

This seminar focuses on the essential skills required for effective digital content creation, online communication, and cloud-based collaboration in modern academic and professional environments. By using Simulation Mode to inspect data packets frame-by-frame across the OSI layers, switching between logical diagrams and physical rack setups, and automating IoT devices with custom rules and code, learners gain the core practical skills needed to earn Cisco digital badges and prepare for networking certifications like the CCNA.

SEMINAR_05 Completed

INTRODUCTION TO GREENHOUSE GAS ACCOUNTING FOR IT

Date: September 9, 2026
Duration: 3 Hours
Organizer: Cisco Networking Academy

Measuring, tracking, and reporting carbon emissions generated by IT infrastructure under GHG Protocol standards.

Key Takeaways

Greenhouse Gas Accounting for IT provides a standardized methodology to measure and manage the carbon footprint of technology systems across direct operations, grid electricity, and the heavily dominant Scope 3 value chain—including hardware manufacturing, e-waste, and cloud providers. By converting activity data like kilowatt-hours and hardware purchases into actionable carbon metrics ($\text{Activity Data} \times \text{Emission Factor}$), IT leaders can optimize data center efficiency, extend device lifecycles, and meet mandatory global climate disclosure regulations.

INTRODUCTION TO MODERN AI

Date: September 8, 2026
Duration: 6 Hours
Organizer: Cisco Networking Academy

This introductory seminar provides a foundational overview of the Internet of Things (IoT) ecosystem and its practical applications across modern industries. Participants explore how physical devices collect, exchange, and process data through interconnected networks to drive smart decision-making.

Key Takeaways

It demonstrated how real-time device telemetry powers automated workflows across industries, provided frameworks for selecting protocols (MQTT, CoAP, LoRaWAN, Cellular) under specific power and bandwidth constraints, and highlighted end-to-end security through endpoint protection, encryption, access control, and over-the-air firmware management.
